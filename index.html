Try transfers against your real budget before you commit them
    How the budget is worked out
    FPL sells a player back to you at your purchase price plus half of any rise since, rounded down — so a player who has gone up £0.3m is sold at £0.1m profit, not £0.3m. The API does not expose purchase prices without logging in, so this planner values your existing players at today's market price. For players you have held since the start that is exact; for one who has risen since you bought them it can be optimistic by a tenth or two. Treat a plan that is within £0.2m of your budget as needing a check on the real site.
