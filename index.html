Test transfers against the real budget
    How the budget is worked out
    FPL sells a player back at your purchase price plus half of any rise since, rounded down. Purchase prices need a logged-in endpoint, so this values your existing players at today's market price. Exact for anyone held since the start; up to a tenth or two optimistic for someone who has risen. Within £0.2m of your budget, check it on the real site before committing.

    What progress means
    FPL does not publish the thresholds that trigger a price change, so progress is an estimate: net transfers this gameweek measured against how many managers own the player, scaled so 100% is roughly where a change becomes likely. Past 100% a move is due, often that night. Ownership change is this gameweek's net transfers as a share of all managers, in percentage points.
