Planner

How plans are scored
Each plan is walked gameweek by gameweek: transfers are applied, the bank and accumulated hits are tracked, and the best legal eleven is fielded every week. The total is the sum of those weeks minus the hits, so two plans are directly comparable over the same horizon. Existing players are valued at today's market price, since FPL's real sell price needs a logged-in endpoint — so a plan within £0.2m of your budget is worth checking on the real site.

What progress means
FPL does not publish the thresholds that trigger a price change, so progress is an estimate: net transfers this gameweek measured against how many managers own the player, scaled so 100% is roughly where a change becomes likely. Past 100% a move is due, often that night. Ownership change is this gameweek's net transfers as a share of all managers, in percentage points.

What the six scores mean
All but Value are expected points per gameweek, which is what lets a keeper and a striker sit in one ranking. Overall averages the next five gameweeks. Short weights the very next match hardest. Long looks across the whole horizon and deliberately ignores form, leaning on the season rate and the underlying numbers instead, so a player in a rough patch with a good run coming still reads well. Value is points per £10m. Differential is what you gain on the average manager once his effective ownership is accounted for. Captain is the doubled return from the next gameweek alone.
